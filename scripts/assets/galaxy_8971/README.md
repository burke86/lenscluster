Style F: luminous observatory
============================

Generated schematic artwork, not astronomical observations. Created with the
built-in image-generation tool for the presentation scaling-relation diagram.
The PNGs have alpha transparency. `style_f_lens.png` includes blue lensed arcs;
`style_f_member.png` is the companion galaxy without arcs. The Python renderer
embeds these raster assets while retaining vector labels and chart elements.

Original lens prompt:

Generate an isolated scientific editorial illustration of ONE elliptical galaxy matching style F "Luminous observatory": refined softly glowing golden-white central bulge, elliptical amber and warm gray stellar envelope with fine stippled star texture, feathered edges, major axis tilted 30 degrees upward to right, axis ratio about 0.55. Two thin elegant luminous cyan-blue disconnected gravitational lensing arcs on opposite outer sides. No spiral arms, no text, no labels, no background objects, no box, no ground, no frame, no full Einstein ring. The galaxy and arcs should fill 80 percent of a square canvas with generous transparent margin. Beautiful realistic yet painterly astronomical illustration, delicate stellar highlights without cartoon star shapes. Must have a genuinely transparent background, with smooth alpha edges rather than a white rectangle, suitable to embed over a white Matplotlib figure. The galaxy light should stay warm cream/gold and gray, no black opaque background.

Companion prompt: retain the same elliptical galaxy and transparent background,
but remove the blue lensing arcs for the population-member icons.
